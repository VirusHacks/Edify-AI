**Edify-AI** is a personalized career navigation platform that works exactly like Google Maps - showing you where you are, defining the best route to your dream job, and dynamically "rerouting" you when market trends shift or you go off-track.

---

## 🚀 The Vision

In a world of infinite tutorials and "AI hype," students don't fail because they lack talent—they fail because they lack **direction**. 

Every platform solves one piece: Udemy sells courses, LinkedIn helps with networking, ChatGPT gives answers. **Edify-AI connects them all** into a single, cohesive journey from "I'm confused" to "I'm hired."

---

## ✨ Core Pillars (Powered by Google Gemini)

### 1. 📍 Dynamic Career Routing (Roadmapper)
*   **Gemini-Powered Analysis**: Beyond keyword matching, Gemini analyzes your resume, GitHub commits, and career goals to build a roadmap that is unique to *you*.
*   **Real-time Rerouting**: If a new technology becomes industry-standard (e.g., Mojo or JAX), Edify-AI updates your roadmap automatically.

### 2. 📚 Instant Learning Modules (CourseGen)
*   **Flash Generation**: Using **Gemini 1.5 Flash**, we generate custom-tailored learning modules in seconds.
*   **Curated YouTube Integration**: Automatically hooks into high-quality technical content, creating a structured curriculum around existing expert resources.

### 3. 💼 Readiness Engine (ATS & Interview)
*   **Vertex AI Agents**: A multi-agent system (coordinated via LangGraph) iterates on your resume until it hits a 90%+ match for specific Job Descriptions.
*   **Multimodal Mock Interviews**: Behavioral analysis and technical Q&A using multimodal input to assess both confidence and competence.

## 🏗️ Architecture: The Multi-Agent Coordinator

We leverage **Google Vertex AI** to orchestrate 6 specialized agents:

1.  **Roadmap Agent**: Career pathing via market trend analysis.
2.  **Course Agent**: Structured content synthesis.
3.  **Resume Agent**: JD parsing and ATS-grade refactoring.
4.  **Interview Agent**: Dynamic technical evaluations.
5.  **Mentor Agent**: Long-term memory and context retention.
6.  **Orchestrator**: The central hub managing **MCP state**.
